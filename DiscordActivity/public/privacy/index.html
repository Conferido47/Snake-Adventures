Player Information

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Browser and Windows players can choose a display name and save visits and completed scores for the game creators' private dashboard, or choose Play offline without reporting. When you play inside Discord and authorize the identify permission, Snake Adventures stores your player identity and run statistics to maintain personal records, a daily streak, and a lifetime leaderboard. If you use the Wardrobe, we also verify Discord cosmetic entitlements and store your equipped skin. You can separately opt in to receive future completed-run results in a private Discord message from the Snake Adventures bot.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to Snake Adventures, including its website and Discord Activity and Windows edition (the "Game"), operated by the Snake Adventures team ("we," "us," or "our").

2. Information We Collect

For online scores in the Discord Activity, we process:

  • Your Discord user ID, username, global display name, and avatar URL.
  • Each completed run's score, level, result, game version, and submission time.
  • Your personal-best score, highest level, completed-run count, current daily streak, and longest daily streak.
  • A random submission identifier used to prevent the same run from being recorded twice.
  • Discord SKU IDs and active entitlement status for Snake Adventures cosmetics.
  • Your equipped cosmetic ID and the time that preference was updated.
  • Whether you have opted in to private completed-run result messages.

Discord provides a short-lived access token after you authorize the Game. The token is held in browser memory, used to validate your identity with Discord, and is not stored in our score database. Browser and Windows named play uses a separate random device token, not a Discord account. It does not grant cosmetic ownership or Discord leaderboard access.

If you choose Play and save results in the browser or Windows edition, we store your chosen display name, platform, game version, visit identifiers and times, completed-run identifiers, scores, levels, results, and aggregate records. A hash of a random player token associates these records with this browser or Windows profile. Names are self-reported and do not verify identity. These records are visible to the game creators through their private dashboard, not the public Discord leaderboard. The browser remembers the name and token in local storage; Windows remembers them in your local application-data folder. You can edit your name at the next launch or choose Play offline to stop reporting for that session. Offline play does not upload visits or scores. Clearing local storage or deleting the Windows player profile creates a new identity on the next named launch; it does not delete existing server records.

3. Discord Activity Information

When launched inside Discord, the Game uses Discord's Embedded App SDK to establish the Activity session. It requests identify for online scores and cosmetic ownership and rpc.activities.write for Rich Presence. The SDK lists available SKUs, opens Discord checkout when you explicitly choose to buy, and signals when an entitlement may have been created. Our backend independently verifies cosmetic ownership through Discord's HTTP API before a paid skin can be equipped. We do not request your Discord email address, server list, messages, contacts, or voice communications.

While you use the Discord Activity, Rich Presence can show the main menu, current or final score, level, elapsed run time, pause state, Phantom power, and Hollow King encounters on your Discord profile. Rich Presence operates independently of the private result-message preference and is subject to your Discord visibility settings.

Discord independently processes information under its Privacy Policy.

4. Private Results and Leaderboard

The authenticated lifetime leaderboard displays up to ten players' Discord display names, personal-best scores, highest levels, and ranks inside the Activity. Your own rank remains visible to you when you are outside the Top 10. The leaderboard does not display Discord user IDs or run histories.

Private result messages are disabled by default. If you turn on DM My Completed Results, the Game attempts to message future completed wins and game-overs to you through the Snake Adventures bot. The embed contains your display name and avatar, score and level, personal best, highest level, current and longest daily streak, completed-run count, and lifetime rank. You can turn the preference off at any time. Delivery can fail if your Discord settings block the message, you block the bot, the bot does not share a server with you, or Discord or the network is unavailable. Manual restarts and runs abandoned before game-over are not messaged.

The Share Leaderboard button opens Discord's own share dialog. Nothing is shared unless you choose a destination and confirm. The shared message includes a link back to the Activity and may include the leading score and your current rank. Private messages and user-confirmed shares are retained according to your settings and Discord's policies.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

The Game does not:

  • Read Discord messages, contacts, server content, or voice communications.
  • Request or store your full payment-card, bank-account, or billing credentials.
  • Use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or tracking cookies.
  • Sell virtual currency, consumable boosts, or gameplay advantages.

Current movement, audio settings, and other live gameplay state remain in temporary browser memory and are cleared when the page or Activity closes.

6. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Authenticate score submissions and associate them with the correct player.
  • Calculate personal records, completed runs, and consecutive UTC play dates.
  • Display lifetime rankings and prevent accidental duplicate or excessively rapid submissions.
  • Send completed-run results privately after you opt in and remember that choice.
  • Display available cosmetics, verify durable purchases, and remember the skin you equip.
  • Secure, maintain, debug, and improve the Game and score service.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable law.

7. Hosting and Technical Data

The Game is hosted through Cloudflare Pages, and online statistics are stored in Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device type, request time, and security data to deliver and protect the service. We do not use this information for advertising.

Cloudflare's processing is described in its Privacy Policy.

8. Retention and Deletion

Player statistics, named-player visits, run-submission records, result-message preferences, and equipped-cosmetic preferences are retained while the applicable online feature operates or until they are no longer reasonably needed. You may request deletion using the contact address below. We may retain limited information when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or resolving disputes. Deleting our equipment row resets the selected skin but does not delete or refund the purchase entitlement maintained by Discord. Deleting our database record also does not automatically delete a private result message, user-confirmed share, or score embed posted before this Policy took effect; those messages are controlled through Discord and their destination.

9. Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. We use it only to respond, provide support, protect the Game, or meet legal obligations. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We share live and final play information with Discord for Rich Presence, share result information when you enable private messages or explicitly use the share dialog, use Discord for checkout and entitlement verification, and use Cloudflare to host the Game and store statistics, message preferences, and equipment preferences. Discord and its payment providers process checkout and transaction information under their own policies. We may disclose information when necessary to operate the Game, comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or work with service providers acting on our behalf. Discord, Cloudflare, and your email provider process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

11. International Processing and Security

Discord, Cloudflare, and email infrastructure may process information in countries other than your own. We use reasonable measures appropriate to the limited information we handle, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

12. Children

The Game is intended only for people old enough to use Discord under Discord's Terms of Service and applicable local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information we hold, or to object to certain processing. We will respond to valid requests concerning stored player statistics, result-message preferences, equipped-cosmetic preferences, and support communications as required by applicable law. Purchase and payment records controlled by Discord must be handled through Discord. We may need your Discord user ID or other reasonable verification before acting on a player-data request.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the Game, its providers, or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised Policy here and update its effective date. Material changes will apply prospectively unless applicable law requires otherwise.
